The Studio

Small team.

Mad Quest Studio is an independent game developer based in Canberra, Australia — a small team focused on building games with depth, personality, and staying power.

We make the kinds of games we want to play: thoughtful, atmospheric, and a little unexpected. Strong mechanics, clear design, and a distinctive voice sit at the core of every project.

Every game we build starts as its own mad quest.
